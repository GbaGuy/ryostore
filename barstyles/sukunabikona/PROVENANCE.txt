Sukunabikona was designed and authored by aethctl in 2026 for the Ryoku
showcase/ricing competition.

The Ryostore edition is derived from the author's standalone v19 prototype. The
store port keeps that approved visual composition while replacing its prototype
Hyprland, MPRIS, PipeWire and palette polling seams with Ryoku's public Wm,
Media, Audio, AudioBars, Sysinfo, StatsFeed, Network, Session and Theme services.

No third-party artwork, code, or remote assets are bundled. The store preview is
made from a real capture of the author's running Ryoku desktop.
